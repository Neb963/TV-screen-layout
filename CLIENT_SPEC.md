# Television News Screen Layout Designer

## Purpose
A simple web app for creating **ultra-high-fidelity** static mockups of television news screens. Designed for professional graphic work—not animation, video editing, or broadcasting.

## Requirements

### Presets and branding
- Many fully editable presets, including Fox News–style layouts.
- Create custom channels with their own logos, typography, and color palettes.
- Include common broadcast elements: lower thirds, headlines, tickers, “LIVE” badges, clocks, and split-screen panels.

### Canvas editor
- Drag, reposition, resize, rotate, crop, and duplicate images and other elements.
- Support precise numeric controls for position, dimensions, rotation, and spacing.
- Provide layers, grouping, locking, stacking order, alignment, snapping, and guides.
- Include zoom, pan, and undo/redo.

### Images and graphics
- Paste images from the clipboard or upload PNG, JPEG, and SVG files.
- Place and replace images in green-screen or placeholder regions, with adjustable cropping and positioning.
- Support channel logos, vector shapes, fills, borders, gradients, shadows, and opacity.

### Text and typography
- Edit all text, including headlines, labels, and ticker content.
- Support font selection and custom font uploads.
- Control weight, size, line height, letter spacing, alignment, outlines, and shadows.

### Projects and export
- Export sharp, high-resolution static images at configurable dimensions.
- Save and import fully editable projects—not just flattened screenshots.
- Preserve layers, assets, text, fonts, styling, and layout when reopening a project.
- Save custom designs as reusable presets.

## Quality criteria
- Fine control over typography, spacing, alignment, and visual effects.
- Crisp text and vector rendering at the chosen export resolution.
- Exported images faithfully match the canvas.
- Projects reopen without missing assets or layout changes.

## Out of scope
Animation, moving tickers, video playback or editing, live data feeds, and broadcasting. All screen elements are static mockup components.
